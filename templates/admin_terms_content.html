Mandatory Admin Agreement

You must read and accept these strict responsibilities before accessing the Moderator Dashboard.

I. The 15 Rules of Moderation

1. Keeping User Data Safe: As an admin, you will see private information like user emails, phone numbers, and addresses. You promise to never save, screenshot, share, or use this information outside of Foundify for any reason.
2. Being Fair and Honest: You must verify lost and found claims fairly. You cannot accept bribes, play favorites, or use your admin powers to bully or scam anyone.
3. No Personal Contact or Side Deals: You must never contact users outside of the Foundify platform for personal reasons, to start relationships, or to try and make side deals. All official communication must stay on the platform.
4. Handling Dangerous or Illegal Items: If you see someone posting lost or found items that are illegal (like weapons, stolen goods, or drugs), you must immediately block the post and notify a Super Admin. Do not ignore it or try to handle it yourself.
5. Professional and Respectful Behavior: You are representing Foundify. You must always act professionally and respectfully. You cannot use abusive language, troll users, or pick fights, even if a user is being difficult.
6. No Fake Accounts or Manipulation: You are strictly forbidden from creating fake user accounts to approve your own claims, boost platform numbers, or manipulate the system in any way.
7. Reporting Bugs and Security Flaws: If you find a glitch, a bug, or a way to hack the platform, you must report it to a Super Admin immediately. You cannot use it to your advantage or share it with anyone else.
8. Keeping Your Account Secure: Your admin account is your responsibility. You must never share your password with friends or family. If someone else gets into your account because you were careless, you will be held responsible for the damage they cause.
9. We Are Watching (Audit Logs): Remember that every time you approve a claim, reject a claim, or block a user, the system saves a permanent record of what you did. Super Admins check these records regularly to make sure everything is safe.
10. You Are a Volunteer: By accepting this role, you understand that you are volunteering to help moderate the community. You are not an official employee of Foundify, and you do not get paid.
11. No Discrimination or Bias: You must treat all users equally. You cannot reject claims or block users based on their race, religion, gender, nationality, or personal beliefs.
12. Do Not Act Like the Police: You are a platform moderator, not a law enforcement officer. If two users are fighting over who truly owns an item, or if someone reports a stolen item, follow platform protocol. Do not try to investigate crimes or play detective yourself.
13. Handling Police Requests: If you are ever contacted by the police, lawyers, or courts asking for user data or information about an item, you must not give them anything directly. You must immediately report it to a Super Admin to handle officially.
14. No Public Defamation (Social Media): Internal platform issues must stay internal. You agree not to go on social media or public forums to leak internal moderator discussions, talk badly about the platform, or damage the Foundify brand.
warning 15. Penalties for Misuse (Please Read Carefully)

If you misuse your admin powers, leak user data, take bribes, or intentionally break any of the rules listed above, you will face the following penalties:

  • Your admin rights will be stripped immediately without warning.
  • Your account and IP address will be permanently banned from Foundify.
  • If you do something illegal (like scamming users or stealing data), we will report you to the police and hand over all your activity logs.
  • If your bad actions cause Foundify to get sued, we will take direct legal action against you to make you pay for all our legal costs, fines, and damages.

II. Admin Privacy Policy

As an administrator, your activity is subject to strict monitoring to ensure the safety of the Foundify platform.

  • Activity Logging: Your IP address, login times, and every single action you take (approvals, rejections, bans, profile views) are logged and tied to your account.
  • Data Handling: You consent to processing user data strictly in accordance with the law. You must not export, scrape, or otherwise transfer user data out of the system.
  • Surrender of Data: In the event of a legal subpoena or criminal investigation into your actions, Foundify will surrender your IP logs, activity history, and communication records to law enforcement without notifying you.

Please close this tab and return to the main dashboard to accept these terms.
