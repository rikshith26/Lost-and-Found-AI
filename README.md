Foundify

Welcome to Foundify! This is a website made to help people find their lost items easily in colleges and communities.

If you lose something or find something, you just upload a picture and a short description. Our system is smart enough to look at the pictures and words to automatically match a lost item with a found item. This saves everyone a lot of time and worry.

Once a match is made, the person who lost the item and the person who found it can safely chat with each other on our website to arrange returning the item.
